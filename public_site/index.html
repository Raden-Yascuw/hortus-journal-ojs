About Journal

The Hortus Journal: Journal of Sustainable Plantation and Agro-Industrial Sciences, hosted by Departemen Agrosains & UPT Perpustakaan Universitas Negeri Malang, is a dynamic Diamond Open Access platform dedicated to exploring sustainable tropical plantation crops, precision farming (IoT & UAV multispectral sensing), and circular agro-industrial sciences. Our journal provides a space for researchers, agronomists, engineers, and practitioners to share valuable insights, evidence-based practices, and innovative strategies that shape sustainable agro-ecosystems.

Journal Description

Hortus Journal: Journal of Sustainable Plantation and Agro-Industrial Sciences (Hortus J. | p-ISSN: 2988-7401 | e-ISSN: 2988-741X) takes open access scholarly communication in tropical plantation and agro-industrial sciences to a modern standard. Published twice a year (June and December) by Departemen Agrosains & UPT Perpustakaan Universitas Negeri Malang, our journal implements a rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review process (2–4 weeks SLA), Zero APC (Diamond Open Access) under CC BY 4.0, and full metadata optimization for Google Scholar, DOAJ, SINTA, and Crossref DOI integration.
